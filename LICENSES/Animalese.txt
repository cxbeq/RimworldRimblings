Animalese Typing voice banks

Rimblings includes eight converted A-Z voice banks from Animalese Typing by
Joshua Sherry (Josh), version 1.45.3:
https://github.com/joshxviii/animalese-typing

The banks were converted from the extension's per-letter AAC clips to 44.1 kHz
mono PCM, trimmed to 150 ms per letter, and level-matched per bank. Rimblings
further varies pitch, cadence, tone and volume during playback.
